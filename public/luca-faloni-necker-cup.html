The Necker Cup

The world's most exclusive six days in tennis.

An invitation-only pro-am on Sir Richard Branson's private Necker Island, held November 29 through December 4, 2026. Guests compete and socialize with tennis legends, founders, principals, cultural figures, and philanthropists in a closed island environment.

Private Access

Closed island, intimate guest count, high-trust introductions, and full-week brand immersion.

Charity Engine

The week benefits Virgin Unite and the National Tennis Foundation, with more than $12,000,000 raised and directed since inception.
